# US daytrade audit — 2026-10-12

## Confirmed and corrected

- US entry reused a pre-open reservation cutoff, blocking regular-session buys. Entry now uses the existing premarket/regular-session checks; broker permission and buying-power guards remain.
- USD order notional was subtracted from KRW allocation. Reserve the complete KRW allocation until the next broker reconciliation instead.
- Submitted/unfilled buys did not consume loop budget or slots. They now do; partial sales do not immediately release cash or slots.
- US allocations skipped the remaining-cash and per-slot caps. Both caps now apply.
- Buying power took the maximum of executable and estimated conversion funds. Only executable funds/quantity are used now.
- Discovered NYSE/AMEX exchange metadata was ignored by the legacy symbol mapping. Prefer discovered metadata for price and order routing.
- Legacy SOXL engine state appeared as a US daytrade position/default despite infinite-buy ownership. Exclude it from the US page's managed-position list without deleting history.
- The US page omitted the lock component's market input. Pass US explicitly so domestic holdings do not appear there; no locks are changed.

## Verification boundary

Broker-free production-method tests cover pending buys, filled buys, budget caps, slots, partial-sale cash handling, regular-session entry, discovered exchanges, and legacy position filtering. Existing test suite also runs. No live orders, cancellations, switches or unlock changes are part of this audit.

## Remaining risks — not a claim of full readiness

- Historical intraday profitability was not remeasured in this audit. Test success does not establish an edge.
- Dynamic discovery uses liquidity/momentum/spread filters, then training/quality gates; passing discovery is not an entry signal. A small curated fallback is still used when scans fail or produce no candidates.
- Legacy exchange defaults still apply to symbols absent from discovery. Durable broker-confirmed symbol/venue metadata is preferable for held positions and manual searches.
- Session helpers check weekday/time, but do not themselves cover holiday/early-close calendars. Broker-layer holiday safeguards and unsupported extended sessions need end-to-end mock coverage.
- Live broker acceptance, partial execution, cancellation and settlement were not exercised. Do not equate local regression coverage with these external checks.

Next architectural step: separate candidate research, execution planning, reservation accounting and broker reconciliation; use a persistent per-order ledger and explicit currency fields rather than relying on loop-local inference. Keep SOXL owned exclusively by infinite-buy.
